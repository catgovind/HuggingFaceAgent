Record of past Wayne Manor party menus, kept by Alfred:

- Last year's villain masquerade ball served Avenger Bites, Batman Brownies,
  and Kryptonite Punch -- guests particularly praised the Batman Brownies and
  requested they return for future villain-themed events.
- The charity gala menu was a 3-course formal dinner: smoked salmon
  appetizer, a mushroom wellington main (for the vegetarian guests), and
  chocolate lava cake for dessert.
- Feedback noted the buffet at casual events runs out of vegetarian options
  too quickly; always prepare extra.
